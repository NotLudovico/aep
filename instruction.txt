## Band structure
- Comparison with results from literature
- Calculation of the Fermi velocity in any point where a band crossed the Fermi energy
- Calculation of the effective mass in any point where a band crossed the Fermi energy, fit by a quadratic behaviour a small regione around the Fermi energy of the band, then you can obtain the effective mass in that k point
- The total average value of v_F and m^* on the Fermi surface, that can be used in other analyses on experimental values can be obtained in first approximation by a simple avergae of the different values found in the different k_F points
- Density of states, comparison with literature, density of states at Fermi energy, Density of charge carriers n is very important for calculations and analysis of the transport properties
- Phonon properties, disperion relation comparing with literature, speed of sound in directions of high symmetry by a linear fit of the acousting branches at energies close to zero, the energy values of optical branches at q=0 can give info on the energies of Raman peaks but attention not all the modes are Raman active
